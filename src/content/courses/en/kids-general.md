Kids General English is a fun, interactive program that helps young learners build strong foundations through age-appropriate lessons, engaging activities, and supportive classroom interaction.

Students develop vocabulary, sentence building, listening, speaking, reading, and writing through activities that encourage participation and confidence. The structured pathway helps each child progress clearly from the basics toward more independent use of English.

## What You'll Achieve

- Speak confidently in English
- Master vocabulary progressively
- Build reading & writing skills
- Prepare for Cambridge YLE exams

## Curriculum

**Pre-Starters:** Letters, numbers, short/long vowels, basic sentences

**Starters:** Letters, numbers, long vowels, basic sentences

**Super Minds 1A–6B:** A1 to B1+ progression through interactive learning

**Reading & Writing:** Parallel development of all four skills

## Program at a glance

- Ages 6–13
- One month and 32 total learning hours per course
- 8 CEFR-aligned levels delivered across 12 courses
- Dedicated sections for boys and girls, supported by female trainers
