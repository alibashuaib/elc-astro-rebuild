Kids General English is a fun, interactive program that helps young learners build strong foundations through age-appropriate lessons, engaging activities, and supportive classroom interaction.

Students develop vocabulary, sentence building, listening, speaking, reading, and writing through activities that encourage participation and confidence. The structured pathway helps each child progress clearly from the basics toward more independent use of English.

## Course Overview

### What Your Child Will Achieve

- Speak with confidence in English
- Build vocabulary progressively
- Develop reading and writing skills
- Prepare for Cambridge YLE exams
- Gain independence in using English

### Program at a Glance

- Ages 6–13
- One month and 32 total learning hours per course
- 14 monthly batches: Pre-Starters and Starters at Level 0, then Super Minds 1A–6B
- Dedicated sections for boys and girls, supported by female trainers

## Course Diagram

**Pre-Starters** – A one-month foundation batch for complete beginners, introducing language sounds, essential vocabulary, and classroom English.

**Starters** – A one-month batch that builds everyday vocabulary and simple sentence patterns.

**Super Minds 1A** – Children begin communicating about familiar topics through songs, stories, and everyday vocabulary.

**Super Minds 1B** – Builds confidence through guided speaking and classroom activities.

**Super Minds 2A** – Expands everyday vocabulary and helps children use longer sentences in familiar situations.

**Super Minds 2B** – Develops listening, speaking, reading, and writing through age-appropriate themes.

**Super Minds 3A** – Extends reading, writing, and speaking tasks with growing independence.

**Super Minds 3B** – Strengthens confidence using language and skills across a wider range of activities.

**Super Minds 4A** – Strengthens accuracy, comprehension, and independent communication.

**Super Minds 4B** – Applies language skills through reading, writing, discussion, and projects.

**Super Minds 5A** – Extends vocabulary, reading, writing, and discussion for more confident communication.

**Super Minds 5B** – Deepens fluency and helps learners use English more independently.

**Super Minds 6A** – Prepares learners for the most advanced reading, writing, and speaking work in the Kids pathway.

**Super Minds 6B** – Consolidates the Kids English pathway and prepares learners for their next stage of study.

## How You'll Learn

### What You'll Work On

- Interactive vocabulary building through games and activities
- Speaking practice with supportive feedback
- Reading comprehension using age-appropriate texts
- Writing simple sentences and short paragraphs
- Listening skills through engaging audio and video
- Grammar foundations through communicative activities
- Building confidence and positive attitudes toward English learning

### Our Teaching Philosophy

Learning English should be fun and engaging for children. Our instructors use play-based and activity-driven instruction, with songs, games, stories, and hands-on activities that keep young learners motivated and excited about English. We create a supportive, positive classroom environment where every child feels encouraged to participate.

### Classroom Features

- **Small class sizes** — small groups for individual attention
- **Age-appropriate materials** — textbooks and activities designed specifically for children
- **Interactive games and activities** — vocabulary games, storytelling, arts and crafts
- **Supportive teachers** — patient, energetic instructors trained in child development
- **Bilingual support** — instruction primarily in English with Arabic clarification when needed for younger learners
