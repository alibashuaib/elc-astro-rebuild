Special Courses extend beyond the standard general-English track. They are organized around specific learner groups, needs, and timetables to provide focused development and more varied learning options.

## What You'll Achieve

- Focused skill development for your specific needs
- Learning tailored to your schedule and goals
- Specialized vocabulary and communication strategies
- Improved confidence in targeted areas
- Flexible learning that fits your lifestyle

## Course Examples

Programs vary by demand and may include:

- **Conversation and Speaking** – Fluency and confidence building
- **Academic English** – University preparation and research skills
- **Professional Communication** – Industry-specific training
- **Exam Preparation** – Target-focused test readiness
- **Intensive Programs** – Accelerated learning over shorter periods
- **Custom Programs** – Tailored to group or organizational needs

## What You'll Work On

- Skills and topics specific to your chosen program
- Small-group or customized instruction
- Flexible scheduling outside standard course times
- Real-world application and practice
- Progress tracking and feedback

Because topics and schedules change, contact ELC for the current course list, dates, entry requirements, and pricing.
