Women's General English follows ELC's structured adult pathway in a dedicated learning section for female learners. Each student begins at the level identified through a placement test and develops speaking, listening, reading, and writing through modern materials and practical classroom activities.

Qualified female instructors guide the learning experience in an environment designed for privacy, comfort, participation, and steady progress. Around 70% of class time is dedicated to practice—including conversation, exercises, activities, and real-life English use.

## What You'll Achieve

- Build English fluency for work and social situations
- Communicate with confidence and clarity
- Master key listening and reading comprehension
- Develop professional and personal writing skills
- Strengthen grammar and vocabulary foundations
- Advance toward higher English proficiency levels

## Program at a glance

- For female learners aged 13 and above
- Dedicated women's section with qualified female instructors
- One month and 40 learning hours per course
- Structured CEFR-aligned General English pathway
- Speaking, listening, reading, and writing

## Course Diagram

**Pre-Fun** Recognize English letters and sounds and begin reading simple words.

**Fun A** Introduce yourself, spell names, and use basic words in short conversations.

**Fun B** Ask and answer everyday questions, describe routines, and read short texts.

**Lint A** Make introductions and plans, describe family, order food, and talk about everyday technology.

**Lint B** Discuss fitness and vacations, shop for clothes, arrange travel, and compare prices.

**Lint C** Read longer texts for main ideas and details, then write clear connected paragraphs.

**Lint D** Share experiences and opinions while handling hotels, transport, and personal care.

**Lint E** Discuss food, personality, art, technology, and everyday ethical choices.

**Hint A** Analyze ideas in longer readings and support your opinions in organized writing.

**Hint B** Speak with greater fluency about culture, health, services, reading, and emergencies.

**Hint C** Express plans and regrets, explain traditions, and debate technology, social issues, and the environment.

**Hint D** Develop confident, detailed conversations about social, travel, and professional situations.

**Hint E** Discuss complex ideas with clearer reasoning and respond naturally to different viewpoints.

## What You'll Work On

- Conversation practice in supportive group settings
- Authentic listening materials from real-world sources
- Reading comprehension strategies
- Writing for academic, personal, and professional contexts
- Grammar and vocabulary in practical use
- Confidence building in English communication
- Cultural context in language learning
