Adults General English helps learners improve step by step, beginning at the level identified through a placement test. The course develops speaking, listening, reading, and writing with updated learning materials, practical teaching methods, and equipped classrooms.

Student participation is central to the experience. Around 70% of class time is used for practice—including conversations, activities, exercises, and real-life English—while instructors use the remaining time for explanation, correction, and guidance.

## Course Overview

### What You'll Achieve

- Communicate confidently in everyday situations
- Participate in professional and social conversations
- Read and understand varied English texts
- Write clearly for personal and professional purposes
- Build vocabulary and grammar for advanced fluency
- Prepare for further English study or exams

### Program at a Glance

- For learners aged 13 and above
- One month and 40 learning hours per course
- 16 stages across four proficiency bands, from A0 to C1
- Additional reading material to support vocabulary, reading, and writing

## Course Diagram

**Pre-Fun** Recognize English letters and sounds and begin reading simple words.

**Fun A** Introduce yourself, spell names, and use basic words in short conversations.

**Fun B** Ask and answer everyday questions, describe routines, and read short texts.

**Lint A** Make introductions and plans, describe family, order food, and talk about everyday technology.

**Lint B** Discuss fitness and vacations, shop for clothes, arrange travel, and compare prices.

**Lint C** Read longer texts for main ideas and details, then write clear connected paragraphs.

**Lint D** Share experiences and opinions while handling hotels, transport, and personal care.

**Lint E** Discuss food, personality, art, technology, and everyday ethical choices.

**Hint A** Analyze ideas in longer readings and support your opinions in organized writing.

**Hint B** Speak with greater fluency about culture, health, services, reading, and emergencies.

**Hint C** Express plans and regrets, explain traditions, and debate technology, social issues, and the environment.

**Hint D** Develop confident, detailed conversations about social, travel, and professional situations.

**Hint E** Discuss complex ideas with clearer reasoning and respond naturally to different viewpoints.

**Advanced A** Evaluate complex texts and write structured arguments with supporting evidence.

**Advanced B** Communicate nuanced ideas confidently in academic, social, and professional discussions.

**Advanced C** Refine fluency, defend complex viewpoints, and adapt your language to different audiences.

## How You'll Learn

### What You'll Work On

- Speaking practice in realistic conversations and role-plays
- Listening comprehension from authentic materials
- Reading strategies for different text types
- Writing for various purposes and audiences
- Grammar structures in practical contexts
- Vocabulary expansion for academic and professional use
- Critical thinking and language analysis

### Teaching Approach

Our instructors use communicative language teaching (CLT), where real-world communication is the goal. Lessons balance structured grammar instruction with authentic conversation practice, ensuring you develop practical language skills you can use immediately in professional and social contexts.

### Our Instructors

ELC's teaching team is made up of certified teachers who bring years of experience teaching adult learners across multiple proficiency levels. Instructors understand the adult learning context—time constraints, professional goals, and practical language needs—and tailor instruction accordingly. Our teachers are passionate about helping you reach your English goals.
