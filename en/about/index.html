Common questions

Clear answers before you begin

01Are ELC certificates accredited?

Yes. Certificates are accredited by official authorities such as the Technical and Vocational Training Corporation (TVTC), depending on the program, and are recognized within Saudi Arabia.

02Do teaching methods change by age?

Yes. Children learn through play, visual tools, and hands-on activities. Adult classes emphasize real-life application, discussion, and confidence in speaking.

03Which curricula and levels are available?

Structured CEFR-aligned curricula build reading, writing, listening, and speaking step by step. A placement test identifies the right starting point, and each course page shows the current program structure.

04Who teaches the classes?

Certified instructors from Arab and international backgrounds are selected for their qualifications, classroom experience, and ability to connect with learners, with regular professional training.
