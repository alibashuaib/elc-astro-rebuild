Terms & Conditions

The current enrollment rules for course fees, attendance, withdrawal, and certificates.

These terms apply to course enrollment and study at ELC. Contact admissions before payment if you need clarification about a course, package, transportation option, or refund eligibility.

  1. Course fees must be paid in full before a trainee begins studying.
  2. Trainees must follow the institute’s standards of conduct. The institute may suspend a trainee for misconduct.
  3. Trainees must use the institute’s furniture, equipment, and learning tools responsibly and may be charged for damage they cause.
  4. A trainee may receive a refund if the registered course is unavailable or if withdrawal is requested at least one week before the course begins.
  5. If a trainee withdraws during the first five days after a course begins, 50% of the course and transportation registration fees will be deducted.
  6. No withdrawal or refund is available after the first five days of the course.
  7. A replacement course book or learning kit costs SAR 170.
  8. A trainee who does not pass may receive a 50% discount when repeating the course within three months of its end date.
  9. Trainees must attend at least 80% of scheduled study hours. Exceeding the permitted absence may affect course completion and certificate eligibility.
  10. Certificates must be collected within three months after the course ends.
  11. A trainee who stops attending is not entitled to a refund.
  12. Continuation after a long interruption or suspension is subject to the institute’s enrollment rules and the validity of the original course period.
  13. A trainee who has stopped studying for more than two months must complete a new level assessment.
  14. If a trainee withdraws from a promotional package, course fees return to the standard non-package rate.
