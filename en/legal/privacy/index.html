Privacy Notice

A clear summary of the information used when you contact ELC through this website.

Information you choose to provide

When you call, email, or message ELC, you may provide your name, contact details, course interest, and information needed to answer your request. The registration form on this site does not save data on the website; it prepares a WhatsApp message that you review and send yourself.

How information is used

Information you send is used to answer enquiries, recommend a course or placement test, support registration, and provide requested educational services.

External services

The site embeds Google Maps and links to WhatsApp, the student portal, and social platforms. These providers may process technical or account information under their own privacy policies when you load or use their services.

Cookies

This site sets no cookies of its own beyond what's needed for it to function. With your consent, given through the banner shown on your first visit, we also use analytics and advertising cookies — including Google Analytics, Google Ads, Meta (Facebook), Snapchat, and TikTok — to understand site usage and measure how visitors reach us — nothing beyond strictly necessary cookies is set until you choose to accept, and you can withdraw consent at any time by clearing your browser's site data for elc.com.sa. Third-party services embedded on this site (Google Maps, YouTube, WhatsApp) may set their own cookies under their own policies when you choose to load or use them.

Retention and sharing

ELC retains communications only as needed for admissions, service delivery, administration, and applicable legal obligations. Information is not sold. It may be handled by service providers used to deliver communications or requested services.

Your questions and requests

To ask about personal information you have shared, request correction, or request deletion where applicable, email info@elc.com.sa or call 059 179 9917.
