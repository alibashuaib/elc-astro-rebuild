Inside the program

A clear, structured learning experience

The Preparation for University Program (PUP) is an intensive English pathway for high school graduates and other learners aged 16 and above. It builds the language skills needed for post-secondary education, training programs, and future work.

The CEFR-aligned program combines the four main English skills with intensive conversation practice, reading and writing development, correct pronunciation, learning technology, and activities that encourage English use beyond the classroom.

Program at a glance

  • Up to 220 hours of practical English learning
  • Speaking, listening, reading, and writing
  • Conversation for everyday, social, and workplace situations
  • Enriching activities, including educational trips and visits when scheduled
  • Additional instructor support when needed
  • Modern learning technology and interactive resources
  • SAR 4,700

FAQs

Who is PUP for?

The program is designed for learners aged 16 and above, especially high school graduates preparing for university, training programs, or future employment.

How intensive is the program?

PUP includes up to 220 hours of practical English learning. Admissions can confirm the current timetable and intake dates.

Which skills are covered?

The program develops speaking, listening, reading, and writing, with intensive conversation practice, pronunciation work, and activities for academic and workplace situations.

Is extra learning support available?

Additional instructor support hours may be provided when needed to help learners address difficulties and strengthen weaker skills.
