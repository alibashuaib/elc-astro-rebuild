## Post Best Practices

### ✅ DO:
- Use location tags (Jeddah)
- Include calls-to-action (Call, WhatsApp, visit)
- Add images/videos when possible
- Use emojis for readability
- Keep language simple and clear
- Include testimonials (real quotes)
- Post at 10 AM or 6 PM (peak engagement)

### ❌ DON'T:
- Excessive promotional language
- False claims or exaggerated statistics
- Spam/multiple posts per day
- Unrelated content
- Poor grammar/spelling
- Ignore comments/questions

## How to Post on Google Business Profile

### Desktop:
1. Go to google.com/business
2. Select your business
3. Click "Posts" on the left menu
4. Click "Create post"
5. Choose post type (standard, offer, event, product)
6. Write content + add image
7. Add call-to-action (button)
8. Schedule or publish

### Mobile (Google Business Profile App):
1. Open app
2. Tap "Posts"
3. Tap "+" (create)
4. Write content + add photo
5. Set call-to-action
6. Publish

### Automation Option:
- **Hootsuite:** Schedule posts to GBP
- **Buffer:** Multi-platform scheduling
- **Later:** Visual calendar planning
